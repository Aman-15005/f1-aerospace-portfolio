About

Engineering Through First Principles

Where computational tools meet experimental validation

I am a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate driven by a passion for research and development in the fields of motorsport, aerospace, and advanced engineering systems. My interests lie at the intersection of engineering design, computational simulations, and applied physics — where innovative ideas are transformed into high-performance solutions.

Through hands-on experience in go-kart engineering, aeromodelling, robotics, and CAD-based product development, I have built a strong foundation in engineering design and analysis. I actively explore CFD, FEA, numerical methods, and optimisation techniques to better understand and predict the behaviour of complex mechanical systems.

My long-term goal is to contribute to cutting-edge R&D efforts that advance vehicle performance, propulsion systems, aerodynamics, and simulation technologies.
